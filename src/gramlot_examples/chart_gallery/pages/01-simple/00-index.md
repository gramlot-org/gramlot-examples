# Simple charts

> **What you learn** — Show parts of a whole with pie and donut charts, and numeric observations with scatter points.

- **Pie and donut:** four prepared regional totals as parts of a whole.
- **Scatter points:** numeric observations without grouping or fitting a model.

Each lesson puts a `frameGrid` with `chart=True` beside the chart on the same Data. Open the chart icon in the toolbar, then Chart settings, to change the type, the measure or the engine. No database is required.
